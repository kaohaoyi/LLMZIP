# Code of Conduct

Keep discussions focused on the code, benchmarks, and bug reports.

## Expected Behavior

- Critique code, designs, and numbers, not people.
- Keep issues and pull requests on-topic and reproducible.
- Give direct, constructive technical feedback.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, slurs, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private information without consent, or unsolicited spam/self-promotion.
- Deliberate disruption of issues, pull requests, or repository threads.

## Reporting

Report violations to **kaohaoyi@gmail.com**. Maintainers may delete off-topic comments, lock threads, or ban repeat offenders from the repository.
